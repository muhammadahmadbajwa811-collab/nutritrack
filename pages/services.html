Features

Six trackers that work together, and a free calculator you can use without an account.

🍛

Meal diary

Log breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for any date. Change servings with plus and minus.

📊

Calories and macros

A progress ring for calories and bars for protein, carbs and fat against your targets.

🚶

Step counter

Manual entry, quick-add buttons, distance, calories and a goal streak. Optional live tracking on phones.

💧

Water tracker

Tap a glass to fill it. Reach your goal and NutriTrack lets you know.

🏃

Workouts and timer

Log running, cycling, gym and more. Calories are estimated from your weight and the time spent.

❤️

Favorites and custom foods

Heart the foods you eat often, and add your own recipes with their own nutrition values.

Free calorie and BMI calculator

Enter your details to see how many calories your body needs. It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, and the results are estimates, not medical advice.

Your daily plan

Fill in the form and press Calculate to see your calorie goal, resting calories, BMI and suggested macros here.
