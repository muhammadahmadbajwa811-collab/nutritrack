    Small habits that help

    💧

    Drink water before meals

    A glass of water before eating can help you feel fuller, and it counts toward your daily water goal.

    🥚

    Start the day with protein

    Eggs, yogurt or daal at breakfast tend to keep you satisfied longer than sugary options.

    🚶

    Walk after you eat

    A ten-minute walk after a meal is an easy way to add steps without planning a workout.

    🍽️

    Check the serving size

    Calories are listed per serving. Two servings means double the calories, so adjust the quantity when you log.
